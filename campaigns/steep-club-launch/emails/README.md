## Every square bracket has to be replaced before this sends

The plan's product facts are all invented. They are in the drafts as labelled
placeholders rather than as sentences, so that a made-up price or a made-up shipping
date cannot reach a reader by accident. The text after each `placeholder:` is the
invented value from the plan's working-numbers table — it shows the length and
register of the real line, and it is not a fact.

Replace all of these. The two cutoff dates first: they are the only ones a reader can
act on and be let down by.

**Dates** — `[EU CUTOFF]`, `[UK CUTOFF]`, `[FIRST BOX TIMING]`, `[NEXT BOX]`

**Money and shipping** — `[PRICE]`, `[GIFT PRICE]`, `[SHIPPING]`, `[BOX SIZE]`

**The teas** — `[TEA 1]`, `[TEA 2]`, `[TEA 3]`, `[TASTING NOTE 1-3]`,
`[BREWING 1-3]`, `[BITTERNESS FIX 2]`, `[HOW MANY WE TASTED]`, `[SOURCING]`

**Terms** — `[PAUSE]`, `[CANCEL]`, `[GIFT RENEWAL]`, `[GIFT PACKAGING]`

`[HOW MANY WE TASTED]` needs a number someone actually counted, or the line comes
out. That reminder lives here now, not inside the sentence in the draft.
`[SOURCING]` says what we know and admits what we don't — keep that shape.

`{{first_name}}`, `{{company_address}}` and `{{unsubscribe_url}}` are different.
Those are merge fields and the sending tool fills them. Leave them alone.
